Privacy Policy – Last Ferry Out

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Developer: Flash Designs · Back to the game page

Last Ferry Out ("the game") is a casual puzzle game for players of all ages. We built it so that it works without collecting personal information. This page explains what the game stores, what it does not do, and how to contact us.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features and no analytics of our own, and we run no server. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Data leaves your device in two cases only: the data Google AdMob handles to show ads (section 4), and a date check with Google's servers that unlocks the daily gift (section 7).

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data on your device only:

  • Your level, coins, helpers, hearts, achievements and play statistics (for example, how many levels you have won).
  • Your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, season, reminders) and the avatar you picked.
  • Whether you are under 13 or 13 and older. On first launch the game asks for your year of birth with a neutral age screen; only the resulting age group is kept, and only on the device. It is used to keep the game suitable for younger players and to choose how ads are requested.

This game data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it completely.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check whether a connection is available.
  • Notifications: to show an optional local reminder when your daily gift is ready. The reminder is created on the device; nothing is sent to us. Android asks you before the first one, and you can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in Android settings at any time.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): used by Google AdMob for players 13 and older only. For players under 13, or whose age is unknown, ad requests are marked as child-directed and the advertising ID is not used for ads.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added automatically by Google's libraries for their background tasks. The game itself does not run in the background.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, storage, location or any other permission.

4. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, an ad SDK that is self-certified under the Google Play Families Ads Program. Most ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward; a short full-screen ad may also appear between levels, never more than once every few minutes.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: the device IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as app launch time and hang rate), and device identifiers (the advertising ID and app set ID). It is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit (TLS).

Players under 13, or whose age is unknown: every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent. No personalized or interest-based ads are shown, the advertising ID is not used for ads, and only ads rated for all ages (rating G) are allowed.

Players 13 and older: AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), and how ads are viewed and tapped, in order to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and see the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Purchases

The game does not sell anything for real money. Coins and helpers are earned by playing.

6. Children's privacy

The game is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads shown to children only use the child-directed settings described in section 4. Links that leave the game (for example, to rate the game) are placed behind a simple grown-up check when a younger player is playing.

7. Third-party services

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform for consent).

Rating the game: players 13 and older may now and then see Google Play's own rating card (Google Play In-App Review), so they can rate the game without leaving it. The rating is handled entirely by Google Play; the game never sees it.

Date check: when the device is online, the game sends a simple request without content to Google's servers (www.google.com) to read the current date from the reply. We use that date only to decide when the next daily gift is available, so that changing the phone's clock does not unlock it. The request contains no account, advertising ID or other identifier added by the game and no game data, and the game does not keep cookies from it. Like any internet connection, it reveals the device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location) and basic technical details of the request to Google, which handles them under the Google Privacy Policy.

The game can be played without an internet connection; while offline, the daily gift, videos and part of the level rewards are not available. The game engine (Unity) does not send analytics or crash reports from the game. The game is distributed through Google Play, which may process information as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Your choices

Players 13 and older can reset or delete their advertising ID in the Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), and, where the consent form applies, change their choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button in the game's Settings. Reminders can be turned off in the game's Settings. Because we keep no data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete; uninstalling the game removes everything it stored.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
